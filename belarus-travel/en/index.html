Belarus Travel

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · yarma.belarus_travel

Belarus Travel is an app for tracking visits in Belarus, keeping travel notes, and managing a personal places catalogue.

Privacy contact: mdadev@yandex.ru

1. Developer and contact

The developer of Belarus Travel is responsible for data handled by the app itself. For privacy, access, correction, or deletion inquiries, email mdadev@yandex.ru and include the app name. Do not send recordings or other sensitive information unless needed.

2. Data and its use

Visit marks for regions and cities, dates, notes, custom places, selected photos, and settings are stored on your device. You choose photos, which the app stores to display your places. This content is not uploaded to the developer's servers or passed to the advertising SDK. The app has no user accounts or app-operated cloud sync.

3. Permissions and access

Selected-photo access is used when you attach a photo to a place. The app does not request device location for visit marks, contacts, or microphone access. Internet connectivity is used for advertising and opening external links; the bundled catalogue is available offline.

4. Advertising and third parties

The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads. Ad requests send network and technical information to Yandex, including an IP address, device information, and an available Android advertising identifier. This is used to serve and measure advertising. We do not sell your user records. Yandex processes advertising data under its own policy; not selling data does not mean no SDK transmission.

5. Advertising controls

Advertising depends on the installed build configuration. The build's consent setting is passed to the SDK. The current interface has no separate personalization switch; we do not claim that changing app settings disables all ad requests.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your custom places and records in the app. To remove all local app data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Any backups or device transfers performed by your operating system are controlled by its settings and may retain separate copies. Local data is retained for your use of the app until you delete it or clear app storage. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.

7. Inquiries and security

App data is protected by the operating system's storage isolation and access controls; this is not a guarantee of absolute security. If you contact the developer, your email and message are used to respond and resolve the issue. Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable obligations. Email is hosted by Yandex. Advertising data is transmitted by the SDK using HTTPS/TLS. Information may be disclosed where legally required; unnecessary user records are not published.

8. Your choices

You can change or remove local data using available app and Android controls. For information the developer receives through your inquiries, contact the privacy email. For advertising data, we can help direct your request to the relevant provider, whose retention and deletion procedures are governed by its policy. Access, correction, deletion, and restriction rights apply as provided by applicable law.

9. Updates

Changes to data handling or SDKs will be reflected in this page and the app's policy. This policy explains app practices; reading it or continuing to use the app does not replace separate consent or a system permission where required.
