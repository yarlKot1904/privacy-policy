Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Dices («Кубики» in Russian) is an app for rolling dice. This policy applies specifically to this app.

Privacy contact: mdadev@yandex.ru

1. Developer and contact

The developer of Dices is responsible for data handled by the app itself. For privacy, access, correction, or deletion inquiries, email mdadev@yandex.ru and include the app name. Do not send recordings or other sensitive information unless needed.

2. Data and its use

To roll dice, the app processes your selected dice parameters and the resulting rolls. The core function does not require you to provide a name, email, or other account information. The developer does not use roll results for ad targeting. The app uses Yandex Ads, so its operation is not free of third-party data transmission.

3. Permissions and access

The dice-rolling function does not require camera, microphone, contacts, or location access. Advertising requires network access; the SDK may use an advertising identifier where available under Android settings.

4. Advertising and third parties

The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads. Ad requests send network and technical information to Yandex, including an IP address, device information, and an available Android advertising identifier. This is used to serve and measure advertising. We do not sell your user records. Yandex processes advertising data under its own policy; not selling data does not mean no SDK transmission.

5. Advertising controls

Advertising data processing depends on identifier availability and applicable consent settings in the installed version. You can limit or delete your advertising identifier in Android privacy settings. Non-personalized ads do not by themselves eliminate technical network requests.

6. Retention and deletion

If preferences or history are saved on your device, clear app storage in Android or uninstall the app to remove all local app data. Any operating-system backups are controlled by its settings. Contact us below with questions about data processed by the advertising provider. Local data is retained for your use of the app until you delete it or clear app storage. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.

7. Inquiries and security

App data is protected by the operating system's storage isolation and access controls; this is not a guarantee of absolute security. If you contact the developer, your email and message are used to respond and resolve the issue. Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable obligations. Email is hosted by Yandex. Advertising data is transmitted by the SDK using HTTPS/TLS. Information may be disclosed where legally required; unnecessary user records are not published.

8. Your choices

You can change or remove local data using available app and Android controls. For information the developer receives through your inquiries, contact the privacy email. For advertising data, we can help direct your request to the relevant provider, whose retention and deletion procedures are governed by its policy. Access, correction, deletion, and restriction rights apply as provided by applicable law.

9. Updates

Changes to data handling or SDKs will be reflected in this page and the app's policy. This policy explains app practices; reading it or continuing to use the app does not replace separate consent or a system permission where required.
