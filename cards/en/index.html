Privacy Policy — Cards: Durak and Thousand

Effective date: October 2, 2026.

App operator: ИП Бушмакин Марк Сергеевич (individual entrepreneur). Privacy contact: npmark101@gmail.com.

1. About the app

Cards offers Durak and Thousand against computer opponents. You can play without an account or an internet connection. Saved matches, settings, and statistics are stored on your device. We do not send match contents to our own server or provide cloud synchronization.

2. Ads and technical data

Some versions of the app display Yandex interstitial ads between completed matches. The advertising SDK connects to the internet to load ads. Yandex may process technical information needed to request and operate ads, including IP address, device and operating-system details, and information about ad impressions and interactions. The exact data depends on the platform, SDK version, and device settings.

The app tells the advertising SDK that user consent has not been obtained and disables location tracking. The app does not request location access or add the Android advertising-ID permission. This does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no technical data. If the device is offline or no ad is available, the games remain playable.

Advertising technology provider: Yandex. Yandex Privacy Policy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/en/.

3. Why data is used

On-device data lets you resume games and retain settings and statistics. Technical data processed by the advertising SDK is used to load and display ads, operate and measure them, and prevent abuse under Yandex's terms. We do not sell saved matches or intentionally pass them to the advertising SDK.

4. Storage and deletion

Saves and settings remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app; a new match replaces the previous save for that game. Uninstalling may remove your game progress. Yandex sets its own data retention periods; deleting local saves does not control them. For questions about data processing or deletion requests, contact us at the address above.

Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include app data in system backups and transfer it to another device. These features are managed by the operating-system provider, not by this app. Backups do not guarantee recovery of your game progress.

5. Third parties

The app does not create an account or upload game saves to our own server. When ads are enabled, technical data may be sent to Yandex for the advertising SDK to work. Information you choose to email to support is used only to reply to your request.

6. Data security

Local data is stored in app storage protected by operating-system access controls. According to the Yandex SDK documentation, the SDK encrypts the data described there in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

7. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or advertising technology changes. The revised version will be available at the same public link and in the app, with an updated effective date.
