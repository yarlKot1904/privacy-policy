Data on your device

The app saves your service dates, country and term, the names and dates of personal events, settings and the photo you choose. It uses these data for the countdown, widget, reminders and appearance. It stores them in the app’s storage and does not send them to the developer or include them in ad requests. The countdown does not require an account.

You choose a photo through the system picker. The app copies it into its own storage. The app uses notification permission for reminders. You can revoke it in Android settings.

Advertising

Versions with advertising load banners through Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver and measure ads and for diagnostics, Yandex may process the IP address, device and operating system details, device identifiers including the advertising identifier when available, and data about interactions with ads. The ad SDK’s access to device location is disabled.

Yandex Privacy Policy
https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/en/

Sharing a card

When you tap Share, the app sends the image to the Android share menu. The card can show your service stage, countdown, discharge date, country and the photo you choose. Further processing depends on the app and recipient you choose.

Storage and deletion

You can delete a personal event from Important Dates and remove the photo from Appearance in Settings. Clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Android may include app data in a system backup or transfer it to another phone. Backup management depends on your device and account settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete system backups or cards you have shared.

Data security

Local data is stored in app storage protected by Android access controls. The Yandex documentation describes encryption of advertising data in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Yandex describes its data retention practices in its privacy policy. Clearing app storage does not delete that data from Yandex.

Inquiries and rights

If you contact the operator, the sender address, message text and attachments are used to review and respond to your request. Email services process messages for delivery and storage. The operator keeps correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

You can use the address above to ask about how your data are processed or to request correction or deletion. If the way the app handles data changes, the updated policy will appear in the app.
