Punktik: Parcel Pickup Sim

Privacy Policy

Published November 8, 2026.

Publisher: ИП Бушмакин Марк Сергеевич (individual entrepreneur). Privacy contact: npmark101@gmail.com.

1. Game data

Punktik simulates operating a parcel pickup point. Parcels, customers, pickup codes, rankings and money in the game are fictional. Progress and settings are stored on your device so you can resume play. The game does not use accounts, cloud synchronization, or separate developer analytics.

2. Advertising and data sharing

The app uses Yandex Ads. When ads are loaded or displayed, Yandex may process IP addresses, technical device and app information, ad impression and interaction events, and the advertising identifier if available under the installed version and device settings. This information is used to deliver and measure advertising. See the Yandex Privacy Policy for its advertising data practices.

3. Data security

Local saves are protected by operating-system access controls. Yandex describes its protection of advertising data in transit in the Yandex SDK documentation.

4. Retention and deletion

Local saves are retained until deleted. To delete progress and settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. If your operating system created a backup, manage it separately through device settings. Uninstalling does not delete information already processed by Yandex; you can contact Yandex or the publisher about that data.

5. Support requests

If you email the publisher, your email address and message are used to respond and retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the address above.

6. Changes

The current policy is available on this page. It will be updated if data practices or the advertising SDK change.
