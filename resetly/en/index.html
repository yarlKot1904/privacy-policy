Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · com.resetly.resetly

Resetly is an app for personal check-ins, abstinence streaks, and support for changing habits.

Privacy contact: mdadev@yandex.ru

1. Developer and contact

The developer of Resetly is responsible for data handled by the app itself. For privacy, access, correction, or deletion inquiries, email mdadev@yandex.ru and include the app name. Do not send recordings or other sensitive information unless needed.

2. Data and its use

Your device stores personal motivation, streak dates, check-ins, daily difficulty, notes, relapse events and reasons, exercise history, XP, achievements, and reminder settings. These records may contain sensitive information about habits and intimate life. They are used only for your personal journal and progress calculations, not sent to the developer's servers or used for ad targeting. There are no user accounts or cloud sync.

3. Permissions and access

Notification permission is used for local reminders at your chosen time. The time zone is used for calendar and reminder scheduling. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access. The advertising SDK may use internet connectivity.

4. Advertising and third parties

The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads. Ad requests send network and technical information to Yandex, including an IP address, device information, and an available Android advertising identifier. This is used to serve and measure advertising. We do not sell your user records. Yandex processes advertising data under its own policy; not selling data does not mean no SDK transmission.

Yandex Privacy Policy · Yandex personal-data inquiries

5. Advertising controls

The current version passes a refusal of personalization to the advertising SDK and disables location tracking. Ads depend on the build's advertising configuration. Sensitive journal entries are not passed to the advertising provider.

6. Retention and deletion

Settings offers a confirmed Delete all data action that removes your profile, history, progress, and app settings. You can also clear app storage in Android or uninstall the app. Automatic cloud backup is disabled in the Android configuration. Local data is retained for your use of the app until you delete it or clear app storage. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data that exists only on your device.

7. Inquiries and security

App data is protected by the operating system's storage isolation and access controls; this is not a guarantee of absolute security. If you contact the developer, your email and message are used to respond and resolve the issue. Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable obligations. Email is hosted by Yandex. Advertising data is transmitted by the SDK using HTTPS/TLS. Information may be disclosed where legally required; unnecessary user records are not published.

8. Your choices

You can change or remove local data using available app and Android controls. For information the developer receives through your inquiries, contact the privacy email. For advertising data, we can help direct your request to the relevant provider, whose retention and deletion procedures are governed by its policy. Access, correction, deletion, and restriction rights apply as provided by applicable law.

9. Updates

Changes to data handling or SDKs will be reflected in this page and the app's policy. This policy explains app practices; reading it or continuing to use the app does not replace separate consent or a system permission where required.
